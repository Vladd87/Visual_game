# Reference photos

**These are for the critic only. Never ship them in the app.** They are what the builder/critic loop judges renders against, including the blind A/B test ("which one is real?").

There are 15 real photographs. About 1,390 candidates were found by search; roughly 700 were downloaded and every downloaded image was looked at. The ~680 Wikimedia hits were screened by title and metadata first, because of the throttling below. All 15 are at least 1600 px on the long side.
- Images are stored as downloaded: no grading, no retouching, no resizing.
- They were re-encoded at JPEG q92 with EXIF/GPS stripped, so no photographer location data sits in the repo.
- The only edit is a crop on ref02 (noted in its row).

`contact_sheet.jpg` is the one-image overview. The five rows marked **[camera]** match the game camera: high, tilted, long lens and/or shallow depth of field.

## How they were found

Everything went through the **Openverse API** (anonymous) and the **Wikimedia Commons API**; Google Images was not used. Scripts are in `tools/refs/`.
- **Openverse.** Searched with the Wikimedia, WordPress Photo Directory and iNaturalist sources. Openverse's Flickr results are 1024 px, below the 1600 px floor, so none were used.
- **Commons API.** Used for targeted searches: tilt-shift river, tōrō nagashi, lantern in rain, Japanese garden rain, fireflies, creek at dusk.
- **Search terms.** mossy stream/creek, wet stones rain, creek long exposure, moss macro, paper/floating lantern, fireflies, tilt-shift, rain ripples, raindrops on water, Japanese garden rain/stream, stream dusk, lantern fog, stone lantern moss, pebbles water, mist forest, puddle reflection, leaf on water.

## Rejected (main reasons)

About 700 images were viewed on contact sheets (all 615 WordPress, 69 iNaturalist, 17 Wikimedia); finalists were checked at full size.
- **Fake tilt-shift.** "Covered Bridge Tilt-Shift" and "Teeny Tiny Colorado" use synthetic blur, oversaturation and vignettes. That's a heavy edit, and a poor "is it real?" benchmark, since the blur itself is fake.
- **Composites.** "Field of fireflies" is stacked with star trails.
- **Watermarks.** A signature on a Tokyo tōrō-nagashi frame.
- **Fake light sources.** An LED "candle" lantern.
- **Off-subject or off-mood.** Lighthouses, aircraft named Firefly, mudcracks, herbarium and microscope shots, bright tourist daytime shots.
- **Low resolution.** Flickr/Openverse 1024 px images.

## Network notes

- `upload.wikimedia.org` and `commons.wikimedia.org` rate-limited this sandbox's shared egress IP: HTTP 429 with `Retry-After` of 120–600 s.
  - I honoured Retry-After in full and paced requests.
  - So I fetched only a prioritised shortlist from Wikimedia, and filled the rest from Openverse's WordPress and iNaturalist sources, which weren't throttled.
- Openverse's thumbnail proxy returns 424 for Wikimedia-hosted items. I used Wikimedia's own thumbnailer instead.
